# P7. Private receiving through ENS

**Verdict:** open

## What we need to know

The receive-slot idea (pre-blinded points in a resolver record) is close to ERC-5564/6538
stealth meta-addresses published through ENS. We need to know what exists, so the slot
design is either built on it or clearly different from it.

## Where to look

- ERC-5564, ERC-6538, and ENS integrations of stealth meta-addresses.
- Past ENS prize winners on privacy or payments.
- ENSv2 docs: per-name resolvers and records meant for payment data.
