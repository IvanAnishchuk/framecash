# P1. Chaumian / blind-signature eCash on EVM chains

**Verdict:** open

## What we need to know

Who has shipped or proposed blind-signature notes redeemable on an EVM chain, and how
redemption gets gas: relayer, fresh funded address, paymaster, or something else. A
design where the pool itself pays gas from the note is the claim to originality here.
